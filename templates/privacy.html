Policy

Last updated: May 2026. Your financial data is personal — here's exactly how Spendly handles it.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic profile information such as your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense entries you log — including amount, category, date, and description. We also collect limited technical data such as login timestamps to help secure your account.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly service: displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and helping you understand your spending patterns. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we never sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our database with industry standard encryption at rest and in transit. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of trusted third party providers for hosting, analytics, and email delivery. These providers process data only as needed to support the service and are bound by their own privacy commitments. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or how we handle your data? Reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe. You can also request a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it entirely.
